How to import the CSVs into CRM

Back up current records. Import family CSV into Accounts and client CSV into Contacts. Choose Update existing records only, match the CRM record ID, and map Amount Invested, Current Value, Gain % and Portfolio As-of Date. Leave name fields unmapped.

Use yyyy-MM-dd dates, keep empty-value protection on, and keep workflows, assignment and follow-up tasks off. Verify import counts and sample records. This converter does not read live CRM records or submit imports.
